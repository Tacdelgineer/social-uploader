Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Social Uploader processes information when an authorized user connects social accounts and publishes content.

Information processed

Social Uploader processes account identifiers, display names, authorization scopes, encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens, post titles and captions, selected publishing settings, delivery status, provider error details, and video and cover files selected by the user. It also receives routine security and request information from Cloudflare.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the user, connect accounts, validate provider capabilities, transfer user-selected content, schedule and track delivery, display operational history, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot failures. Social Uploader does not sell personal information or use connected-account data for advertising.

Sharing

Information is sent only to the providers selected by the user—YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok—as needed to perform the requested publishing action. Cloudflare provides hosting, access control, temporary object storage, and metadata storage. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and retained until disconnection, revocation, or expiration. Temporary media is deleted as soon as all selected deliveries succeed or a job is cancelled. Media for a partial or failed delivery is retained for no more than 24 hours for retry; abandoned staging media has a seven-day fallback cleanup. Delivery metadata and security logs are retained only as needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

User choices

Users choose which platforms receive each post and may disconnect a provider from the dashboard. Disconnecting removes the locally stored authorization and attempts provider revocation. Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their stored information by contacting the operator.

Security

The private dashboard and APIs are protected by Cloudflare Access. Provider secrets and OAuth tokens are not exposed on public pages or in review diagnostics. No security measure is absolute, but access controls, encryption, minimal scopes, and short media retention reduce risk.

Contact

Privacy questions and data requests may be sent to the Social Uploader operator through Tacdelgineer on GitHub.
